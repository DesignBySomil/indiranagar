# Indiranagar reference sheet (research, Oct 21)
Sources: OSM extract in repo (public/data/world.json, raw /tmp/osm.json), Times of India (https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/bengaluru/bengaluru-after-1-5-years-of-work-indiranagar-100-feet-road-carriageway-is-ready/articleshow/98320673.cms), Citizen Matters (https://citizenmatters.in/residents-inconvenienced-by-ongoing-works-at-indiranagar/), Wikipedia Indira Nagar metro station, BangaloreMapped 100 Feet Road.
## Roads
- OSM: 100 Feet Road lanes=2 oneway=yes segments (the two carriageways, each one-way, 2 lanes); 12th Main lanes 2; most residential roads have no lane tag. Model: 2-way roads, one lane each way on side streets; 100 Feet Road as two carriageways with a narrow concrete divider. No green medians, no cycle lanes.
- 100 Feet Road was rebuilt under BBMP TenderSURE and white-topped (concrete), so surface is pale concrete with patches and cracks, not black asphalt.
- Footpaths: broken, uneven, obstructed by parked bikes and cars, construction mud (TOI, Citizen Matters).
## Bridges and transit (OSM, bbox Indiranagar)
- 5 footbridges (highway=footway, bridge=yes), 2 road bridges (3rd Cross Rd, Sai Baba Mandira Rd tertiary bridge=yes), Baiyappanahalli Rotary Flyover (under construction), Indiranagar Metro Station building plus station node (12.97833, 77.63866), Swami Vivekananda Road station node (12.98593, 77.64490). Purple Line is elevated on pillars along CMH Road. Proposed elevated corridors are only proposed: do not build.
## Look
- Flat-roofed concrete 3-5 storey buildings, painted shutters, big Kannada+English signboards, water tanks on roofs, overhead wires, rain trees, parked scooters, dusty warm light, auto-rickshaws (black-yellow/green-yellow), two-wheelers dominate, buses (BMTC blue/white), Yulu bikes.
